Privacy

Last updated 4 October 2026

MIKŌ runs in your browser. By default your tasks never leave the device you typed them on, and there is no account to create and no server holding your data.

What is stored on your device

Everything you create — tasks, projects, labels, comments, attachments, settings and the activity log — is written to IndexedDB in your browser, under the name miko. A small amount of session and preference data is kept in local storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere unless you deliberately turn on syncing, described below.

Clearing your browser's site data for this domain deletes all of it permanently. There is no copy for us to restore, because we never had one.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics script, no tracking pixel, no advertising network, no session recording, and no error-reporting service. The site sets no cookies of its own.

The one third-party request

The page loads two typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). That request tells Google your IP address and basic browser information, as any request to any server does. Google states it sets no cookies for this. Nothing else on the page contacts a third party.

If you turn on syncing

Syncing is off unless you enter your own Supabase project details under Settings → Account, or the site has been deployed with them configured. If it is on:

  • your email address is used to create and sign in to an account;
  • your tasks and attachments are copied to that Supabase project;
  • the data sits in your own database, under your own control, governed by Supabase's terms rather than ours.

Signing out ends the server session. Local data stays on the device unless you choose "Sign out and forget me", which erases it.

Children

MIKŌ is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since we collect nothing at all by default, there is nothing to delete; if syncing is on, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because your data is on your device, you already have direct access to it. Settings → Data exports everything as CSV, JSON, a calendar file, or a full backup, and deletes it on request. If syncing is on, the same applies to the copy in your Supabase project.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the change is noted in the app's release notes.
